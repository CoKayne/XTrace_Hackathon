# Howard Marks Public Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Product role

This pack is a discipline layer for consensus, price, uncertainty, cycles,
contrarianism, and process evaluation. It is not a Howard Marks persona and
does not generate a hidden imitation of his reasoning.

## Important VC adaptation

The system must preserve two conclusions independently:

1. **Company conclusion** — whether the startup can create durable value.
2. **Investment conclusion** — whether the security, price, capital path, and
   fund context offer an attractive distribution of outcomes.

A strong company can be an unattractive deal at a demanding price. A weak
consensus can create an opening, but unpopularity alone is not evidence of
mispricing. A funding boom or contraction changes financing and exit
assumptions before it changes product evidence.

## Material disagreement preserved

Marks's defensive tradition and venture power-law portfolio logic are not
averaged into one style. `MMI-10` requires the system to identify whether the
fund's strategy depends on loss minimization, extreme winners, or a stated
combination. The card may expose a conflict; it may not silently select a
universal posture.

## Interfaces to the main system

- Evidence Pack: consensus snapshot, company evidence, terms, scenario model,
  financing conditions, and counterevidence.
- Framework Registry: all cards are independent and weight zero.
- Deterministic services: valuation, ownership, dilution, runway, payoff
  distributions, and portfolio contribution.
- Disagreement layer: company quality, price/value, cycle position, and fund
  posture remain separate.
