## VC transfer boundary

Marks's core practice developed in credit, distressed debt, and public-market
value investing. The following transfers require explicit qualification:

- A startup financing price is negotiated and episodic, not a continuously
  observable market price.
- Early-stage intrinsic value is a broad scenario distribution, not a stable
  point estimate.
- A venture portfolio may require rare extreme winners; pure loss minimization
  can destroy its return model.
- Capital-market cycles affect financing availability, dilution, runway,
  customer budgets, and exits, but do not by themselves prove company quality.
- Contrarianism is not a sector keyword or an instruction to oppose consensus.
