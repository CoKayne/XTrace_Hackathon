# Sequoia / Valentine / Moritz Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This pack preserves three distinct layers:

1. Sequoia's public institution doctrine.
2. Direct public statements by Don Valentine or Michael Moritz.
3. Product synthesis and revealed behavior, explicitly labeled as such.

It does not create a composite Sequoia personality and does not claim access to
private investment reasoning.

## Required sequence

1. Define the customer problem and the narrow value proposition.
2. Build market scenarios from problem prevalence, willingness to pay,
   adoption, expansion, and change—not a legacy threshold.
3. Test customer pull and product evidence, including negative cases.
4. Evaluate founder purpose and learning through observed work and customer
   outcomes, not identity or performative intensity.
5. Test whether the company updates quickly as customer and market evidence
   changes.
6. Reconcile runway, unit economics, financing risk, and future investment
   needs.
7. Search outside convenient categories without rewarding novelty by itself.
8. Revisit passed deals only when specified evidence or conditions change.
9. Match holding horizon to company prospects, fund constraints, and liquidity
   obligations.
10. Evaluate investor value-add as a governed capability, not a brand promise.

## Main safeguards

- Every card runs independently and may disagree with the others.
- Sequoia institution doctrine is never silently attributed to Valentine or
  Moritz.
- Cisco, Google, Zappos, LinkExchange, PayPal, and POPSUGAR are selected cases,
  not a training set or causal proof.
- The historical one-billion-dollar market reference is not a current rule.
- Founder obsession is not a personality, demographic, pedigree, charisma,
  hours-worked, or self-neglect score.
- Frugality does not authorize harmful underinvestment, unsafe working
  conditions, or indiscriminate cuts.
- A passed deal is revisited only because evidence changed, not because a
  founder persisted or a category became fashionable.
- Investor operating involvement requires consent, competence, role clarity,
  conflicts review, and a bounded exit plan.
- No card independently creates valuation, expected return, Invest Candidate,
  or a success probability.

## Product interfaces

- Evidence Pack: customer interviews, lost-deal records, product usage,
  retention, contracts, billing and cash, pipeline and backlog, market
  scenarios, competitive evidence, founder decision logs, staffing and
  operating plans, financing documents, runway, board records, fund-life and
  liquidity constraints.
- Deterministic services: market-scenario arithmetic, cohort retention,
  concentration, gross and contribution margin, burn, runway, cap table,
  waterfall, ownership, dilution, and valuation.
- Narrative layer: evidence, applicable rule, judgment, counterevidence,
  unknowns, limited conclusion, and next evidence request.
