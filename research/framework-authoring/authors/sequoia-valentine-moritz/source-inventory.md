# Source Inventory — Sequoia / Don Valentine / Michael Moritz

Research cutoff: 2026-07-28

## Attribution rule

- `institution_doctrine`: authored by Team Sequoia or presented as a Sequoia
  program/framework.
- `person_direct`: words spoken directly by Don Valentine or Michael Moritz in
  a first-person transcript or attributed oral-history excerpt.
- `revealed_behavior`: a disclosed investment or operating action, without
  inferring a universal rule from the outcome.
- `product_synthesis`: a product-safe bridge across independently attributed
  claims. It is not presented as Sequoia's, Valentine's, or Moritz's doctrine.

Later Sequoia material is not back-attributed to Don Valentine or Michael
Moritz. Sequoia portfolio stories are treated as selected retrospectives.

## Excluded or constrained uses

- No later Sequoia doctrine is represented as a private or timeless
  Valentine/Moritz method.
- No portfolio-company result is used to estimate success probability.
- No personality, school, demographic, location, charisma, social presence, or
  working-hours score is created.
- The historical market-size number in *Elements of Enduring Companies* is
  retained only as source context, never as a current threshold.
- The 2022 downturn deck is not a mandate to cut indiscriminately.
- The product does not imitate a named investor or expose private chain of
  thought.
