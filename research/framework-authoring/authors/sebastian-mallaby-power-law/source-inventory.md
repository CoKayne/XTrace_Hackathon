## Attribution boundary

Mallaby is a historian and analyst of venture capital, not the source of one
unified investment doctrine. Cards therefore distinguish:

- `historical_description`: reported firm practices and industry evolution;
- `empirical_association`: observed distributions or research findings;
- `product_inference`: a bounded workflow proposed by this product.

No Sequoia, Benchmark, Accel, Kleiner Perkins, Tiger Global, or individual
investor is said to endorse a product rule merely because the book describes
an episode involving them.

## Rights posture

Only public metadata, public sample locators, short public interview concepts,
and neutral paraphrases are used. No chapter text, long quotation, private
interview material, or simulated investor reasoning is reproduced.
