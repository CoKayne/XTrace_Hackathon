# The Power Law Public Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This is a **historical-pattern pack**, not a Sebastian Mallaby persona or a
claim that one formula reproduces elite venture investors. It helps the system
ask portfolio, experimentation, prepared-mind, network, institutional, and
governance questions.

## Anti-survivorship rule

Every historical case used in a report must carry:

- the decision-time evidence known then;
- the comparison set or missing base rate;
- at least one failure or counterexample;
- a statement that retrospective success does not prove predictive validity;
- no formal decision weight unless prospectively validated.

## Main system interfaces

- `PL-01` and `PL-02`: deterministic portfolio and ownership services.
- `PL-03`: milestone and evidence-request planner.
- `PL-04`: market event / prepared-mind opportunity map.
- `PL-05`: XTrace pass-reason memory and revisit workflow.
- `PL-06`: sourcing provenance and network-bias audit.
- `PL-07`: value-creation plan with named owner and capacity.
- `PL-08`: team/institution evidence.
- `PL-09`: governance and founder-power dissent.
- `PL-10`: case-transfer guardrail applied before any historical analogy.

## Material conflicts preserved

- Heavy-tail logic does not prove that the system can identify the tail.
- Broad experimentation conflicts with concentrated ownership and finite
  partner capacity; both conclusions remain visible.
- Founder autonomy can accelerate company building while weak governance can
  destroy value.
- Network strength can improve access while narrowing who gets considered.
