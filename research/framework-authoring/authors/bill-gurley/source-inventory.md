## Version and transfer risks

- Company examples and numerical multiples are historical observations, not
  current thresholds.
- Public-company DCF intuitions require adaptation before use on young private
  companies.
- Marketplace examples reflect particular eras and geographies; liquidity and
  take-rate economics must be recomputed from current company data.
- “Red flags” are investigative prompts, not fraud classifications. Jurisdiction,
  corporate location, founder sales, and control structures require
  case-specific legal and governance review.
- Gurley's portfolio disclosures create potential selection and confirmation
  bias in company examples.

## Rights boundary

Cards contain neutral paraphrases and exact section locators. No long quotation,
archived chart, proprietary Benchmark material, or investor-persona prompt is
included.
