# Bill Gurley Public Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This pack converts Gurley's public analytical writing into ten independent
lenses for revenue, unit economics, growth, marketplaces, take rate, market
expansion, private-round diligence, financing/liquidity risk, and governance.

## Required sequence

1. Reconcile revenue definition, cash collection, gross margin, and true
   contribution margin.
2. Separate average historical economics from marginal cohort and channel
   economics.
3. Audit every LTV/CAC assumption, owner, interaction, and sensitivity.
4. For marketplaces, prove both-side pull and local or segment liquidity.
5. Evaluate take rate against participant value, bypass, multihoming, and
   competitive response.
6. Build TAM from behavior-changing use cases and explicit scenarios.
7. Apply public-company-like scrutiny to late-stage private financial claims
   without pretending a private company is already public.
8. Model last-round repricing, burn, capital availability, preferences,
   dilution, and liquidity.
9. Aggregate governance warnings only as diligence triggers and preserve
   legitimate explanations.
10. Keep all valuation, cap-table, cohort, concentration, and scenario
    calculations deterministic and reproducible.

## Main safeguards

- Historical multiples and company examples are not current thresholds.
- Revenue-quality traits inform cash-flow questions; they do not mechanically
  set a multiple.
- LTV is not strategy, and LTV/CAC is not valid without cohort maturity and
  marginal analysis.
- Marketplace checklist totals are not success probabilities.
- High take rate can reflect high verified value; low take rate can still be
  uneconomic.
- Expanded TAM is a scenario, not a base-case entitlement.
- A large private round does not confer IPO-level verification.
- Last-round valuation is not value, liquidity, or realized return.
- Governance warnings never authorize automated allegations of fraud or
  wrongdoing.
- Jurisdiction, founder liquidity, dual-class structures, and nonstandard
  metrics require context, documents, legal review, and human judgment.
- No card independently creates Invest Candidate or a valuation premium.

## Product interfaces

- Evidence Pack: audited and management financials, revenue-recognition policy,
  cohort ledger, channel attribution, retention, unit economics, contribution
  costs, marketplace supply/demand metrics, take-rate history, TAM assumptions,
  cap table, financing documents, governance artifacts, board minutes, audits,
  related-party disclosures, and liquidity plan.
- Deterministic services: gross-to-net reconciliation, cohort LTV, CAC/payback,
  incremental margin, concentration, marketplace density, take rate, runway,
  cap-table waterfall, and scenario valuation.
- Narrative layer: evidence, applicable rule, judgment, counterevidence,
  unknowns, limited conclusion, and next evidence request.
