# Obviously Awesome 2e Public Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This pack evaluates the coherence and evidence behind a B2B company's
positioning and go-to-market hypothesis. It is not a copywriting generator or
product-market-fit score.

## Required sequence

1. Decide whether the product is launched and whether the output is positioning
   or only a positioning thesis.
2. Establish customer audience, offering scope, champion, and cross-functional
   evidence owners.
3. Identify real current competitive alternatives from customer behavior.
4. Identify capabilities differentiated against those alternatives.
5. Translate capabilities into evidenced customer outcomes.
6. Identify characteristics of customers who care most.
7. choose a category that supplies useful context.
8. Test in live best-fit sales conversations.
9. Preserve falsifiers and revisit triggers.

## Main safeguards

- Do not use an LLM-generated competitor list as customer evidence.
- Do not begin from desired category or future vision.
- Do not confuse features, generic benefits, and differentiated value.
- Do not call pre-launch assumptions demonstrated positioning.
- Do not use investor pitch evidence as a substitute for buyer evidence.
- Do not equate positioning clarity with product-market fit.
- Do not add a valuation premium from a qualitative positioning score.

## Fidelity boundary

The author published a detailed 2026 podcast mini-series aligned to the second
edition. It directly supports most revised doctrine. The full paid edition and
subscriber workbook remain required to verify exact step wording,
multi-product decision rules, examples, and template semantics.

## Product interfaces

- Evidence Pack: win/loss, sales calls, shortlist data, product capability,
  proof of customer outcomes, customer segments, category assumptions.
- Market events: can create a repositioning trigger but cannot establish
  current customer behavior.
- Deterministic services: funnel, win/loss, pricing, retention, CAC, revenue,
  segment, and valuation metrics only.
