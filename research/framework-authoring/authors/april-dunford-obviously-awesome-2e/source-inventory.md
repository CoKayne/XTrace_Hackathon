## Access boundary

The complete updated book and subscriber workbook were not treated as public
full-text sources. The author's 2026 podcast mini-series gives strong direct
support for the revised pre-work, competition, value, category, and post-work
sections. Licensed review remains necessary for:

- the exact five-step wording and sequence in the final book;
- the complete multi-product decision tree;
- all book examples, templates, and Positioning Canvas field definitions;
- any material present only in the paid book or subscriber workbook.

## Product boundary

This pack evaluates whether a company has evidence-based B2B positioning and a
testable go-to-market hypothesis. It does not:

- write advertising copy;
- treat a founder pitch as customer positioning;
- infer competition from an LLM's general knowledge;
- declare product-market fit;
- convert positioning quality directly into valuation;
- attribute a product-synthesized VC diligence score to April Dunford.

## Rights posture

Use neutral paraphrases only. Do not reproduce the book, workbook, canvas,
slides, transcripts, or long quotations.
