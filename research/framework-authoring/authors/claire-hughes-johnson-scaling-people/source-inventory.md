# Source Inventory — Claire Hughes Johnson / Scaling People

Research cutoff: 2026-07-28

## Version finding

The framework is anchored to the first Stripe Press edition of *Scaling
People: Tactics for Management and Company Building*, published in March 2023.
The official book page describes a 480-page practical guide with more than 100
pages of worksheets, templates, exercises, and example documents.

The complete paid book was not treated as public full text. Public evidence is
drawn from:

- an official Stripe Atlas excerpt;
- the Stripe Press launch interview;
- author interviews and a transcript-backed Stanford talk;
- bibliographic records for version control.

## Access boundary

The public materials support the pack's main concepts, but licensed review is
still required for:

- exact chapter and page locators;
- the complete hiring, candidate-review, onboarding, performance, and exit
  procedures;
- definitions and semantics of the book's worksheets, scorecards, templates,
  and exercises;
- any prescribed thresholds, role-specific checklists, or examples available
  only in the paid edition.

No protected worksheet or template is copied into this pack.

## Product boundary

This pack is an operating-diligence lens. It assesses whether a company has
stage-appropriate, observable mechanisms for alignment, hiring, management,
feedback, and accountability. It does not:

- declare a company good because it has more process;
- infer culture from slogans or founder personality;
- score psychological traits or protected characteristics;
- treat hiring pedigree, headcount growth, or employee sentiment as company
  quality;
- replace employment-law, HR, or organizational-psychology review;
- add a valuation premium from a qualitative operations score.

## Rights posture

Use neutral paraphrases only. Do not reproduce book templates, exercises,
