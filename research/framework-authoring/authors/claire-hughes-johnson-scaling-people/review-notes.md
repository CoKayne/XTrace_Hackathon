# Scaling People Public Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This pack converts public author material into an operating-diligence lens for
venture investing. It asks whether the company's people and operating
mechanisms are appropriate for its current complexity and whether those
mechanisms produce observable alignment, learning, accountability, and
results.

## Required sequence

1. Establish stage, traction, team size, complexity, and the specific operating
   problem.
2. Avoid penalizing a small team for not having late-stage infrastructure.
3. Review founder and leadership self-awareness only through observable
   decisions and complementary staffing, not personality inference.
4. Compare stated mission, goals, principles, and charters with actual
   decisions.
5. Map planning, review, decision, metric, and communication cadences.
6. Review hiring evidence, references, onboarding outcomes, and adaptation.
7. Review delegation, feedback, performance, fairness, and no-surprise
   expectations.
8. Connect operating mechanisms to delivery, customer, financial, quality, and
   retention outcomes.
9. Preserve counterevidence and request the next observable artifact.

## Main safeguards

- Stage-appropriate lightweight process is preferable to process volume.
- Product-market fit, customer value, and business outcomes remain separate.
- Do not infer culture from polished values, employee sentiment, or public
  employer branding.
- Do not infer psychological traits, protected characteristics, or future
  performance from text, images, voice, or demographic proxies.
- Hiring and performance conclusions require human review, privacy controls,
  structured evidence, and bias monitoring.
- Founder centrality can be rational at an early stage; distinguish productive
  involvement from an unacknowledged bottleneck.
- Direct feedback cannot excuse abusive conduct or lack of psychological
  safety.
- Headcount growth is not progress.
- No card carries a valuation premium or formal investment weight.

## Fidelity boundary

Public author-direct material strongly supports the high-level operating-system,
founding-document, complementary-team, scaled-management, hiring-diligence, and
feedback concepts. A licensed copy is still necessary to verify the complete
hiring, onboarding, performance, exit, worksheet, and template procedures.

The pack deliberately omits any exact book template, scorecard, candidate
review rubric, or performance-calibration procedure that cannot be verified
from public author material.

## Product interfaces

- Evidence Pack: organization chart, goals, dashboards, decision log, meeting
  artifacts, role definitions, hiring packets, reference notes, onboarding
  plans, engagement data, performance records, and outcome metrics.
- Market events: can change hiring plans, operating risk, and required cadence
  but cannot prove internal capability.
- Deterministic services: headcount and retention trends, delivery cadence,
  quality incidents, customer metrics, financial performance, and workforce
  cohort analysis only after privacy and fairness review.
- Narrative layer: must show evidence, applicable rule, judgment,
  counterevidence, unknowns, limited conclusion, and next evidence request.

## Open review items

- Review the licensed 2023 book and record chapter/page locators.
- Verify complete hiring, onboarding, performance, and exit procedures.
- Define employment-data privacy, access, retention, and audit controls.
- Obtain employment-law and organizational-psychology review.
- Backtest operating judgments against prospective business outcomes.
- Complete Framework Fidelity and Decision Utility review.
