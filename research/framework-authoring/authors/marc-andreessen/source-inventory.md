## Version risks

- The archive is a later a16z-hosted compilation of historical posts. PDF page
  locators refer to the current 195-page archive, not the original blog.
- The 2018 interview was edited and condensed and was reprinted from Elad Gil's
  *The High Growth Handbook*. Attribution is therefore `coauthored_work`.
- The 2020 and 2023 essays are normative public arguments, not validated
  company-selection formulas.
- The 2025 podcast page provides an episode description but not a full
  transcript; it is corroborating only.
- Site content carries a16z's own warning that posts are informational,
  time-bound, and not investment advice.
