# Marc Andreessen Public Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This pack turns public Andreessen material into ten auditable research lenses:
PMF, market pull, adaptive planning, timing, risk reduction, capital
sufficiency, enterprise dependency, distribution, post-PMF company building,
and a guarded software/build thesis bridge.

## Required sequence

1. Establish stage, business model, customer, market, and product evidence.
2. Separate product-user fit, product-market fit, and broad-market reach.
3. Version the initial plan and record which evidence changed it.
4. Decompose timing into technical, ecosystem, customer, and execution clocks.
5. Convert category-specific risks into testable milestones without hiding
   residual or newly created risk.
6. Model financing needs with runway, milestones, contingency, dilution, and
   funding-window sensitivity.
7. Treat large-enterprise opportunities as probabilistic until contractual and
   economic evidence exists.
8. After PMF, evaluate distribution, next-product capability, and company
   infrastructure independently.
9. Keep macro software/build conviction separate from company proof, deal
   terms, and valuation.
10. Preserve counterevidence and request the next observable artifact.

## Main safeguards

- No “market is everything” shortcut can erase customer evidence, execution,
  governance, terms, or price.
- Early-adopter enthusiasm is not automatically broad PMF.
- Adaptive planning is not permission for unrecorded or random pivots.
- Risk reduction must show residual and transferred risk.
- Historical financing guidance does not specify a universal raise size.
- A large enterprise logo, pilot, or verbal commitment is not booked revenue.
- Distribution advantage and network effects require current durability
  evidence.
- Software transformation is a macro thesis, not a sector bonus.
- “Build” and techno-optimist claims are explicitly normative and receive zero
  formal decision weight.
- Externalities, safety, labor effects, regulation, and customer harm remain
  first-class counterevidence.
- No founder personality scoring or persona simulation is permitted.
- No card independently generates a valuation premium or Invest Candidate.

## Fidelity boundary

The a16z-hosted archive offers strong primary support for the historical PMF,
risk, enterprise-selling, financing, and planning cards. Later author essays
and the edited 2018 interview support timing, post-PMF, software, and build
themes.

The 2025 podcast page is corroborating only because a full transcript is not
available on the official page. The two PMF qualification sources are
a16z-affiliated and must never be displayed as Andreessen's own words.

## Product interfaces

- Evidence Pack: cohort retention, usage, revenue, pipeline, sales cycle,
  customer interviews, market sizing, competitive evidence, technical
  readiness, dependencies, risk register, milestone history, cap table,
  financing plan, enterprise contracts, distribution metrics, product roadmap,
  and operating artifacts.
- Market events: may change timing, demand, distribution, capital availability,
  and regulation but cannot prove company execution.
- Deterministic services: cohort math, market sizing, runway scenarios,
  financing sensitivity, concentration, conversion, CAC/payback, and valuation
  remain separate calculations.
- Narrative layer: evidence, applicable rule, judgment, counterevidence,
  unknowns, limited conclusion, and next evidence request.
