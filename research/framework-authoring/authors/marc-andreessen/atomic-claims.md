# Atomic Claims — Marc Andreessen Public Frameworks

### MA-CLM-001
Andreessen defines product-market fit as being in a good market with a product
that can satisfy that market.

### MA-CLM-002
PMF should be tested through market pull such as usage, customer value,
word-of-mouth, revenue, sales-cycle compression, and demand that strains
delivery; no single generic metric proves it.

### MA-CLM-003
An enthusiastic early cohort may establish product-user fit without
establishing a sufficiently large or accessible market.

### MA-CLM-004
A large, urgent market can absorb product and team imperfections, but it does
not excuse missing customer proof, integrity, execution, economics, or price.

### MA-CLM-005
An initial startup plan is necessarily uncertain and should evolve rapidly as
the team receives market evidence.

### MA-CLM-006
Plan adaptation should preserve the underlying hypothesis, evidence that
changed, and the route toward a large market and PMF rather than reward random
pivoting.

### MA-CLM-007
Commercialization timing has at least four separable dimensions: whether the
technology works, whether the surrounding world is ready, whether a first
customer responds, and whether the team can commercialize.

### MA-CLM-008
Repeated historical failure of an idea may reflect being early, but does not
itself prove that the present attempt is timely.

### MA-CLM-009
Startup risk can be decomposed into category-specific layers such as founder,
market, competition, timing, financing, marketing, distribution, technology,
product, hiring, and location risk.

### MA-CLM-010
Each important risk should be converted into observable evidence or a milestone
that reduces uncertainty while residual and newly created risks remain visible.

### MA-CLM-011
Before PMF, a financing plan should fund a credible path to PMF; after PMF, it
should fund exploitation of the opportunity and a path to profitability, with
contingency for adverse surprises.

### MA-CLM-012
Underfunding can prevent recovery from ordinary setbacks or expose the company
to a closed financing window.

### MA-CLM-013
Excess capital can encourage premature hiring and loss of urgency, while
fundraising itself is not evidence of customer or product success.

### MA-CLM-014
In enterprise selling, a large opportunity is not closed until a binding
agreement and payment or equivalent commitment exist.

### MA-CLM-015
Large-enterprise deals involve many internal stakeholders, irregular timing,
and dependency risk; experienced enterprise selling and continued attention to
the core business reduce but do not remove those risks.

### MA-CLM-016
After PMF, one major task is to capture the reachable market through effective
distribution rather than assume a better product sells itself.

### MA-CLM-017
Early adopters are a small portion of most markets, so their buying behavior
does not establish an efficient route to the broader customer base.

### MA-CLM-018
Distribution can become an advantage, but scale or network effects are not
automatically durable and can unwind.

### MA-CLM-019
After PMF, a technology company must continue developing the next product
rather than treat one successful product as permanently sufficient.

### MA-CLM-020
Post-PMF survival also requires finance, people, legal, marketing,
communications, recruiting, and other company-building competence.

### MA-CLM-021
Andreessen's 2011 software thesis predicts software-driven transformation
across sectors and associates leading companies with growth, margins, and
defensibility.

### MA-CLM-022
The same essay requires companies to delight customers, establish an advantage,
and justify valuation; the macro thesis is not company-level proof.

### MA-CLM-023
“It's Time to Build” and “The Techno-Optimist Manifesto” are normative public
arguments about technology, markets, supply, and institutional capacity, not
empirically validated security-selection rules.

### MA-CLM-024
For product use, macro technology conviction must remain separate from
company-specific adoption, economics, defensibility, governance, externalities,
regulation, deal terms, and valuation.

### MA-CLM-025
PMF can be lost; it is a stage-dependent state requiring continuing cohort and
market evidence rather than a permanent label.

### MA-CLM-026
No Andreessen public thesis in this pack independently establishes an
investment recommendation or valuation premium.
