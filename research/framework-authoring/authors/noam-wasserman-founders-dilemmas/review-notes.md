# The Founder's Dilemmas Framework Pack — Review Notes

Status: `draft / unpublished`

## Core role

This pack helps an investor investigate path-dependent founder, team,
governance, and succession risks. It is not a founder personality model.

## Required sequence

1. Establish the venture stage and current organization.
2. Reconstruct consequential founding choices and their original context.
3. Compare roles, equity, authority, and incentives with current reality.
4. Review conflict, departures, hiring, financing, and board history.
5. Separate explicit founder goals from inferred motives.
6. Identify current misalignment, adaptive mechanisms, and disconfirmers.
7. Keep team judgment independent from market, product, and valuation lenses.

## Main safeguards

- An equal split is not automatically bad.
- Friends, family, strangers, or coworkers are not categorical verdicts.
- A founder remaining CEO is not automatically a negative signal.
- Giving up control is not automatically value-maximizing for the founder.
- Research associations must not become automatic valuation adjustments.
- Missing documentation reduces confidence; it does not prove dysfunction.
- No psychometric or personality inference from biography, speech, or control.

## Fidelity boundary

Public primary sources support the pack's principal claims. The complete book
was not publicly available. Cards on timing, hiring, detailed investor options,
and the integrated diligence bridge require licensed chapter review before
publication.

## Product interfaces

- Evidence Pack: cap table, founder agreement, vesting, roles, board,
  employment and departure terms, financing history, conflict examples.
- Independent lens execution: preserve disagreement with founder-market-fit,
  power-law, founder-control, and governance lenses.
- Deterministic services: cap-table, vesting, ownership, voting, board-control,
  and scenario calculations only.
