turn population-level associations into deterministic investment decisions.

## Important access boundary

The complete 2012 Princeton University Press book is not publicly accessible in
full through the sources reviewed. Public material is sufficient for a
well-sourced research draft, but it is not sufficient to claim complete
chapter-level fidelity for:

- Chapter 2 timing and career-capital details;
- Chapter 5 role allocation;
- Chapters 7–8 hiring and compensation;
- the full Chapter 9 investor-option comparison;
- Chapter 11 synthesis and model details.

Cards touching those areas use `product_synthesis` or reduced fidelity and
remain unpublished until licensed-book review.

## Rights posture

- Use only neutral paraphrases and short bibliographic descriptions.
- Do not redistribute book pages, transcripts, cases, or figures.
- Keep statistical findings attached to their population, period, and study.
- Do not label an AI output as “Noam Wasserman's decision.”
- Do not infer a founder's private motivation from control terms alone.
